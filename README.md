# Cockapoo Responder 🐶

An interactive canine acoustic responder and soundboard designed specifically to capture a Cockapoo's curiosity, evoke head-tilts, encourage playful responses, and woo him into singing along.

Built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and the **Web Audio API** for zero-latency, realistic procedural canine audio synthesis.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

You can run the app with one command using Python:

```bash
cd /Users/benz/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/cockapoo-responder
python3 server.py
```

Or simply open `index.html` directly in your favorite browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.):

```bash
open /Users/benz/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/cockapoo-responder/index.html
```

## 🐾 Key Features

### 1. 🐶 Emotion-Based Canine Barks & Vocalizations
Cockapoos have distinct vocal communication styles. The app synthesizes realistic barks with adjustable pitch, tempo, and formants:
- **Happy Bark**: Bouncy double yip with bright formants.
- **Excited Puppy Yip**: Rapid high-pitched staccato chirps.
- **Curious "Boof"**: Muffled, inquisitive low whuff that reliably evokes the famous Cockapoo head-tilt.
- **Sad Whimper**: High-frequency descending whine with gentle vibrato (triggers maternal/protective interest).
- **Watchdog Alert Bark**: Crisp, authoritative bark.
- **Playful Growl & Yip**: Low rumble with 14Hz amplitude flutter that sweeps up into a cheerful chirp.

### 2. 🧸 Tactile Squeaky Toys & Rodent Chirps
- **Super Squeaker Banner**: Press and hold to compress the toy inward; release to hear the exit intake chirp!
- **Classic Rubber Duck**: Dual-tone squeaker.
- **Rapid Multi-Squeak**: 4-burst excitement squeaks.
- **Mouse / Rodent Chirp**: 8–11 kHz ultrasonic-adjacent chirps (taps into prey/play drive).
- **Wheezy / Punctured Toy**: Raspy air-hiss squeak.
- **Tennis Ball Bounce**: Resonant rhythmic boings.

### 3. 🎶 "Woo Him" Melodic Engine & Pack Howl
- **"AWOOO" Pack Howl**: Starts low, slides smoothly up to the canine harmonic sweet spot (~490 Hz) with warm 5.4 Hz vocal vibrato. Cockapoos are vocal dogs and love lifting their heads to "roo-roo" along!
- **Curious Slide Whistle**: Rising and falling glissando whistle that triggers instant head tilts.
- **"Roo-Roo" Harmonized Choir**: Warm vocal chords in welcoming musical intervals.
- **Calming 432 Hz Lullaby**: Relaxing pentatonic chimes scientifically known to soothe and relax canines.

### 4. 🔔 Attention Cues & Curiosity Triggers
- **Ding-Dong Doorbell**: Classic Westminster two-tone chime.
- **Treat Bag Crinkle**: Granular high-pass noise burst of snack packaging.
- **Playful Kitten Meow**: Formant-filtered meow.
- **Dog Whistle (11.5 kHz & 16.5 kHz)**: High-frequency pulses audible to dogs.

### 5. 🎛️ Live Acoustic Tuning Controls
- **Pitch Range Slider**: Seamlessly tune from Deep Dog (0.6x) to Cockapoo (1.0x) to Tiny Puppy (1.8x).
- **Speed / Tempo Slider**: Speed up or slow down rhythms.
- **Room Echo / Reverb**: Add room ambience.
- **Master Volume**: Master limiter with soft compressor to protect sensitive canine ears.
- **Quick Presets**: "Puppy Mode", "Standard Cockapoo", and "Big Dog".

### 6. ⚡ Auto Combos ("Woo Modes")
- **The Head-Tilt Extravaganza**: Slide whistle → mouse chirp → boof → squeak.
- **Playtime Frenzy**: Fast squeaks → puppy yips → tennis ball bounce.
- **Sing-Along Pack Howl**: Harmonized choir → AWOOO howl → gentle whimper.
- **"What Was That?!"**: Treat bag rustle → doorbell → meow → boof.

### 7. 🎨 Animated Cockapoo Avatar
- Canvas-rendered curly-haired Cockapoo that tilts his head, perks his ears, opens his mouth, and howls along with the sounds!
- Tap him on the screen to pet him and watch him pant happily.

### 8. 📊 Reaction Tracker
- Log whether your dog **Tilted Head**, **Perked Ears**, **Barked Back**, **Howled Along**, or **Ran Over**.
- Tracks his "#1 Favorite Sound" so you always know what sound works best!
